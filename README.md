# 🛒 Amazon Sales Data Analysis & Visualization

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project performs an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an Amazon sales dataset containing 50,000 customer transactions[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). The objective is to evaluate revenue drivers, payment preference distributions, regional sales performance, and product rating consistency using Python's data science ecosystem[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

---

## 📊 Key Highlights & Insights
- **Revenue Distribution:** Aggregated total revenue across major consumer categories including Beauty, Books, Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, and Sports[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
- **Payment Trends:** Visualized customer payment preferences, showing a balanced distribution across UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Wallets, and Cash on Delivery[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Evaluated rating spreads across product categories using statistical measures, histograms, and box plots[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
- **Regional Breakdown:** Analyzed sales volume and total revenue generated across key geographic markets[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
