#  Amazon Sales Data Analysis & Visualization

## Executive Summary
This project performs an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on an Amazon sales dataset containing 50,000 customer transactions. The objective is to evaluate revenue drivers, payment preference distributions, regional sales performance, and product rating consistency using Python's data science ecosystem.

---

##  Key Highlights & Insights
- **Revenue Distribution:** Aggregated total revenue across major consumer categories including Beauty, Books, Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, and Sports.
- **Payment Trends:** Visualized customer payment preferences, showing a balanced distribution across UPI, Credit Cards, Debit Cards, Wallets, and Cash on Delivery.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Evaluated rating spreads across product categories using statistical measures, histograms, and box plots.
- **Regional Breakdown:** Analyzed sales volume and total revenue generated across key geographic markets.

---
